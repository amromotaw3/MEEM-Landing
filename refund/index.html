Refund Policy

Last Updated: August 29, 2026

1. Overview

MEEM offers subscription plans that unlock premium features within the MEEM local media player utility application. We are committed to being fair and transparent regarding our refund practices.

MEEM is a software utility tool, not a content provider. We do not provide, host, or supply any media content. Your subscription unlocks additional software features and capabilities within the application.

2. 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee on all first-time subscription purchases. If you are not satisfied within 14 days of your initial purchase, you may request a full refund — no questions asked.

To be eligible:

  • The request must be submitted within 14 calendar days of the original purchase date.
  • The request must be for the first subscription purchase on your account (not renewals).
  • Your account must not have been suspended or terminated for a Terms of Service violation.

3. Subscription Renewals

Subscription renewals are generally non-refundable. However, if a renewal charge was processed in error or you were unaware of an upcoming renewal, please contact us within 48 hours of the charge and we will review your case individually.

We recommend cancelling your subscription at least 24 hours before your renewal date to avoid being charged for the next billing period.

4. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will not be granted in the following circumstances:

  • Refund requests made after the 14-day guarantee window has passed (for first-time purchases).
  • Accounts terminated due to a violation of our Terms of Service.
  • Requests citing inability to access third-party content — MEEM is a BYOC (Bring-Your-Own-Content) player utility and does not supply or guarantee availability of any third-party streams or services.
  • Partial refunds for unused time remaining on an active subscription period.

5. How to Request a Refund

To submit a refund request, please contact us via:

  • Email: support@meem-app.com
  • Include your registered email address, order/transaction ID, and a brief description of your reason.

We aim to process all refund requests within 5–10 business days. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method.

6. Chargebacks & Disputes

We ask that you contact us directly before initiating a chargeback with your bank or payment provider. Unauthorized chargebacks may result in immediate account suspension. We are always willing to work with you to resolve any billing concerns fairly.

7. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Refund Policy at any time. Material changes will be communicated to active subscribers. Continued use of MEEM after any update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
