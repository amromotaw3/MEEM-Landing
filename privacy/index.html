Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Your privacy is paramount at MEEM. This Privacy Policy outlines how information is collected, used, and protected when you interact with the MEEM application and landing services.

2. Offline-First & Local Storage Architecture

MEEM is built on a local-first philosophy. Your media files, local library index, watch history, and personal PIN-protected vault remain strictly stored on your local device storage. We do not transmit or store your local media files on external servers.

3. Account Authentication & Data Collected

When you create an account or sign in via OAuth providers (such as Google or Discord), we collect minimal account details required for authentication:

  • Email address
  • Account username and display avatar
  • Unique account identifier and device session bindings

This information is used strictly to manage your account access, device synchronization, and profile preferences.

4. Third-Party OAuth & Integrations

If you choose to log in using Discord or Google OAuth, authentication is handled securely by the respective providers. MEEM does not collect or store your social account passwords.

5. Security & Data Protection

We employ industry-standard encryption, secure HTTPS connections, and strict database access controls (RLS policies) to protect your account data against unauthorized access.

6. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You have full ownership of your data. You may request account deletion or data removal at any time through the application settings or by contacting support.
