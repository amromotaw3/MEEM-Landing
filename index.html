ABOUT THIS PLATFORM

Why this page?

We built this central website so our community has one clean home for everything. Instead of juggling scattered Discord links, announcements, or separate accounts, you have a single place where our apps, events, and community connect together.

Tournaments, Game Nights & Giveaways

Whenever we host an event in the community — whether it's a Minecraft server tournament, a community gaming competition, or a casual weekend game night — everything is coordinated right here.

Having your account connected makes it simple to register for competitions, check event schedules, and join community giveaways for free MEEM App VIP subscriptions, custom discord roles, and exclusive discounts.

One Account for Your Community & App

You don't need separate logins for each thing. Your account here connects your community standing with your MEEM App setup.

You can check your status in the community, see your linked permissions, and directly view your synchronized items like "My List" from the MEEM App so your favorite media is always accessible.

Organized Tools for Staff & Moderators

Behind the scenes, our staff and moderators use this platform to manage registrations, organize tournament brackets, distribute giveaway rewards, and keep rules clear.

Having clean tools helps us run things smoothly and focus on what matters most: creating a fun, welcoming, and active community for everyone.
